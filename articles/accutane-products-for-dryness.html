Products to Help With Accutane Dryness

Accutane can leave skin and lips feeling very dry. Here are a few products to look into, from moisturizing basics to lip care and sunscreen.

A quick note: Dryness and sensitivity can vary a lot from person to person. This is a product guide, not medical advice. Follow your prescriber's instructions and ask your dermatologist or pharmacist before changing your skincare routine, especially while taking prescription medication.

Sun protection

For the look of old marks

ScarAway Silicone Scar Sheets

Silicone scar sheets are marketed for certain raised scars. They are not a treatment for every kind of mark, and they should not be used on broken or irritated skin. Ask your dermatologist whether silicone products make sense for your situation.

  • ScarAway single size
  • ScarAway assorted sizes
Before you buy:

During treatment, keep your routine simple and check with your care team about severe dryness, cracking, irritation, or any product that causes burning. Product needs vary, so the most expensive option is not automatically the best one for you.

Final thoughts

When skin is dry, a few gentle basics may be more useful than adding lots of new products at once. Compare ingredients, start carefully, and use your dermatologist's guidance to decide what belongs in your routine.
